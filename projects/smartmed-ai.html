SmartMed-AI

AI-Powered Doctor Matching & Telehealth Platform

An AI-assisted doctor-patient matching platform that connects patients with the right specialist in seconds. Keyword-based symptom analysis maps complaints to the correct specialization, patients browse verified doctors and book consultations, and an offline AI chatbot auto-engages and emails doctors so no inquiry is missed.

Main Features

From symptom triage to booked consultation — with no inquiry left behind.

psychology

AI Symptom Matching

Keyword-mapping engine translates natural-language symptoms into the correct specialization (e.g. chest pain → Cardiologist).

search

Doctor Discovery & Filters

Search and filter doctors by specialization, city, consultation type, availability, and minimum experience.

event_available

Appointment Booking

Patients pick date, time slot, and consultation type (Online/Physical) with instant booking confirmation.

medical_services

Doctor Dashboard

Doctors confirm/cancel appointments, toggle live availability, and update their professional profile.

assignment_ind

Patient Dashboard

Patients track every appointment by status (pending, confirmed, cancelled) in one unified view.

smart_toy

AI Chatbot Auto-Reply

When a doctor is offline, an automated chatbot replies instantly and collects the patient's information.

mail

Email Notifications

Doctors get an email alert for every new patient message via Nodemailer + Gmail SMTP.

star_rate

Rating & Reviews

Patients rate doctors after a consultation to build trust and surface top specialists.

verified_user

Firebase Authentication

Secure email/password auth with role-based access control (Patient vs Doctor).

account_circle

Profile Picture Upload

Doctors upload a profile photo handled via Multer and served from the backend.

devices

Responsive UI

A custom, framework-free design system that adapts across desktop, tablet, and mobile.

Technology Stack

Node.js Express.js Firebase Auth Firestore Multer Nodemailer Vanilla JS HTML5 / CSS3
