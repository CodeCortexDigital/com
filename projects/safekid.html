Smart Student Transport Safety Assistant

A mobile-based student transport platform connecting drivers, assistants, and parents through QR-based pickup and drop tracking, live GPS monitoring, notifications, messaging, billing, and smart transport assistance.

Tech: Flutter, Firebase, Firestore, FCM

Main Features

Three roles, one goal — a reliable chain of custody from home to school and back.

switch_account

Driver, Assistant & Parent

Single Flutter app with role-based screens — driver owns the van, assistant scans, parents track. Phone OTP and Google login via Firebase.

qr_code_2

Student & QR Management

Add students, assign parents, auto-generate a QR card per child, and export/share the card as a PDF — no printed hardware needed.

route

QR Pickup / Drop Lifecycle

Scan at pickup, school arrival, return boarding, and home drop — each event logs student, time, GPS, and trip for a full safety record.

location_on

Live Van Tracking & ETA

Real-time GPS sharing on a Google Map, parent live-tracking view, and AI ETA prediction so parents know when to step out.

notifications

Parent Notifications

Firebase Cloud Messaging alerts at every event — boarded, reached school, return trip, safely dropped — plus delay and absence notices.

forum

Driver ↔ Parent Messaging

In-app chat for delays, reminders, and handoff notes, with AI quick-message templates (traffic, vehicle issue, absence) generated automatically.

receipt_long

Billing & Fee Status

Monthly fee setup with paid/pending status for parents and a collections dashboard for drivers — with AI fee reminders.

star_rate

Feedback & Ratings

Daily 1–5 star ratings (safety, punctuality, behaviour) feed a driver score, with AI summaries of common praise and complaints.

smart_toy

AI Assistant & Smart Alerts

Rule-based chatbot answers "where is the van?" / "was Ali picked up?" / "fee status?", plus automated notifications, driver performance scoring, and route optimization.

Technology Stack

Flutter Dart Firebase Auth Cloud Firestore Firebase Cloud Messaging Google Maps Google Gemini
